Input mode

Your own tools

Sites where OpenTriage may type or open indicators without asking — your SIEM, XDR, ticketing and wiki. Everything else (and always well-known external services such as VirusTotal) asks first, because the indicator leaves your organisation. Sites are added when you tick "one of our own tools" on an approval.

Screenshots

Lets OpenTriage see pages the way you do (process trees, graphs, icon-only buttons) and click by position. Chrome only allows an extension to capture a tab when it holds the "all sites" permission, so this is a separate, optional grant. OpenTriage still only reads and acts on tabs in your OpenTriage tab group.

Site access

Sites OpenTriage may read (granted from the side panel). Trusted sites can also be navigated to without asking.

Indicator enrichment sources

Off by default. Looking up an indicator sends it to that third party, and each lookup still asks for your approval. Use {ioc} in the URL template.
